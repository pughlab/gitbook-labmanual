# OICR sample abbreviation

B a benign tumor

R a reference or non-tumour, non-disease tissue sample. Typically used as a donor specific comparison to a diseased tissue, usual a cancer

P a primary tumour

X a xenograft derived from some tumour. Note may not necessarily be a mouse xenograft. If it is a mouse, track strain would be useful

M a metastatic tumour&#x20;

C a cell line derived from a tumour

F fibroblast cells

E endothelial cells

S serum from blood where the clotting proteins have been removed

A cells taken from the ascites fluid

T an unclassified tumor, but tumor as opposed to a normal or reference tissue.

O Organoid
